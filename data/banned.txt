# banned.txt — words that must never appear in a Crossword Club fill (or in a generated clue).
#
# One word per line (letters only; case and spaces are ignored), '#' starts a comment.
# Read by scripts/build-wordlist.mjs (npm run wordlist): every word listed here — and its plain plural /
# 3rd-person "-s" form, generated automatically — is removed from data/wordlist.txt and from the clue banks.
# The build also rejects anything containing an unambiguous root such as FUCK or SHIT (see scripts/data/banned.mjs),
# so compounds are covered even if they are missing here. -ED / -ING forms are NOT generated: list them explicitly.
#
# The goal is a friendly puzzle that passes the "breakfast test": slurs, profanity, crude sexual or bodily terms and
# drug slang are out. Ambiguous everyday words (COCKPIT, SNATCH, POT, WEED, GRASS, SCREW, BALLS) are deliberately
# NOT listed — ban them for your own puzzles via the builder's word list (data/user-words.txt: "-WORD").
